10 Essential Health Tips

From our orthopaedic experts — simple habits that make a big difference

1

Regular Exercises

Regular exercise promotes weight control and keeps you physically active. It helps burn calories, strengthen muscles, and supports joint health for a pain-free life.

2

Diet Tips

  • Eat a healthy, balanced diet
  • Consume less salt and sugar
  • Reduce intake of harmful fats
  • Eat plenty of colourful seasonal fruits & vegetables
  • Drink plenty of water, juices & coconut water
  • Do not skip breakfast
3

Breathing

Breathe in and breathe out deeply whenever you remember. Deep breathing reduces stress, improves oxygen flow, and relaxes both the body and mind.

4

Postures

Learn how to sit, stand, move, and exercise in a balanced manner. Correct posture reduces neck and lower back pain and helps maintain long-term spinal health.

5

Walking

Wake up early and go for a walk. Spend time outdoors in fresh morning air, allow your body and mind to relax, and enjoy the natural benefits of movement.

6

Check Blood Pressure Regularly

Regular blood pressure monitoring helps detect health issues early, preventing serious complications and supporting long-term heart and vascular health.

7

Have Regular Check-Ups

Regular health check-ups detect medical conditions early and prevent serious complications. Timely screening ensures better long-term health and peace of mind.

8

Avoid Harmful Alcohol Use

Limiting alcohol protects your liver, heart, and overall health. Reducing harmful alcohol use improves immunity, mental well-being, and quality of life.

9

Get Enough Good Sleep

Quality sleep is essential for physical and mental health. It helps the body repair itself, boosts immunity, improves concentration, and reduces stress levels.

10

Do Not Smoke

Avoid smoking to protect your lungs, heart, and overall health. Quitting smoking improves breathing, circulation, bone density, and life expectancy significantly.

Bone & Spine Health

A complete nutrition and lifestyle guide designed by our orthopaedic experts to keep your bones and spine strong at every age.

Nutrition That Builds Strength

  • Calcium for strong bone density
  • Vitamin D for better absorption
  • Protein for muscle repair & growth
  • Proper hydration for joint health

💪 Calcium

Milk, ragi, sesame seeds, leafy greens

☀️ Vitamin D

Sunlight, eggs, mushrooms, fatty fish

🥚 Protein

Dals, eggs, lean meat, nuts & seeds

💧 Hydration

Water, buttermilk, coconut water

Foods to Limit

  • Excess salt & processed foods
  • Sugary drinks & fizzy colas
  • Too much caffeine
  • Deep-fried, oily foods
