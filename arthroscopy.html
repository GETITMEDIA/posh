ARTHROSCOPY AND SPORTS INJURY

Advanced Care for Active Lives

Welcome to Our Department

At THE POSH – Pondy Ortho Specialty Hospital, our Arthroscopy and Sports Injury Department is committed to restoring mobility, strength, and confidence to athletes and active individuals of all ages. We offer cutting-edge, minimally invasive treatments for sports-related injuries and joint problems—helping patients return to peak performance safely and quickly.

Our Services Include

Arthroscopic (Keyhole) Surgeries

  • Knee (ACL, PCL, Meniscus repair)
  • Shoulder (Rotator cuff tear, recurrent dislocation)
  • Ankle, Elbow & Hip arthroscopy

Sports Injury Management

  • Sprains, ligament tears, tendon injuries
  • Muscle strains and overuse injuries
  • Dislocations and cartilage damage

Rehabilitation & Physiotherapy

  • Sports-specific rehab programs
  • Strengthening and flexibility training
  • Post-operative recovery support

Preventive Sports Medicine

  • Injury prevention strategies
  • Fitness assessment and performance optimization
  • Custom braces and supports

Why Choose Us?

  • Minimally Invasive Surgery – Faster healing, smaller scars, and quicker return to sports
  • Advanced Imaging & Diagnostics – MRI, DEXA Scan, CT, Ultrasound, and on-site Digital X-rays
  • Multidisciplinary Team – Experienced arthroscopy surgeons, sports medicine experts, and certified physiotherapists
  • Personalized Care Plans – Tailored treatment and rehab for each patient's goals and sport

"Whether you're a professional athlete or a weekend warrior, we're here to get you back in action with confidence and care"
