About Us

Welcome to THE POSH

Experts in Advanced Orthopaedic Care — Puducherry's most trusted multispeciality hospital.

Founded in 2005 as Pondy Ortho Clinic and rebranded in 2017 as THE POSH, we are a leading private surgical hospital in Puducherry, committed to delivering high-quality, ethical, and affordable surgical care. Our founder Dr. Veerappan is a renowned Orthopaedic Surgeon with expertise in complex trauma, spine, and joint replacement surgeries.
