THE POSH - Pondy Ortho Speciality Hospital Pvt. Ltd.

“Our mission is to restore mobility and improve lives through evidence-based orthopaedic treatment, advanced surgical care, and compassionate rehabilitation.”

THE POSH is a recognised leader in orthopaedic surgery in the region, offering a wide range of specialties in bone, joint and spine care. Founded in 2005 as Pondy Ortho Clinic and rebranded as THE POSH in 2017, we have steadily expanded our capabilities—investing in modern diagnostics, minimally invasive surgery and post-operative rehabilitation programs for every patient we serve.

About Us

Advanced Orthopaedic Care You Can Trust

Our orthopaedic team delivers expert care for fractures, joint injuries, spine disorders, sports trauma, and complex bone conditions. With decades of surgical experience and a patient-focused approach, we provide dependable diagnosis, advanced treatment options, and personalised rehabilitation support for every individual.

The POSH Hospital @ Kalpakkam

POSH Hospital @ Kalpakkam is a dedicated Emergency & Trauma Day-Care Centre located on ECR Road. The centre is equipped for rapid orthopaedic assessment and immediate management of fractures, sports injuries, joint dislocations and accident trauma.

  • Day-care procedures for fracture management and wound care
  • On-site digital X-ray and essential diagnostics
  • Experienced trauma team supported by The POSH clinical protocols
