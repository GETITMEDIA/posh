Our Expertise

Comprehensive orthopaedic and spine care delivered by expert surgeons, specialists, and physiotherapists working collaboratively for the best outcomes.

Joint Replacement Surgery

  • Total Knee Replacement (TKR)
  • Total Hip Replacement (THR)
  • Shoulder and Elbow Replacement

Spine Care & Surgery

  • Non-surgical solutions for back and neck pain
  • Robotic Spinal Decompression Therapy (SDT)
  • Treatment for disc prolapse & sciatica
  • Minimally invasive spine surgery (MISS)

Trauma & Fracture Management

  • 24/7 emergency care for accidents and injuries
  • Complex fracture fixation and rehabilitation

Arthroscopy & Sports Injuries

  • Keyhole surgery for ligament tears and joint disorders
  • Treatment of ACL, meniscus, shoulder dislocations

Paediatric Orthopaedics

  • Clubfoot correction and congenital deformities
  • Growth-related orthopaedic issues

Bone & Joint Infections

  • Advanced care for osteomyelitis
  • Septic arthritis and post-surgical infections
