Community Health & Awareness Programs

As part of our commitment to social responsibility and preventive healthcare, The POSH – Pondy Ortho Speciality Hospital actively conducts health awareness programs and free medical camps across schools and communities.

Health Awareness Program – Fathima Higher Secondary School

7th August 2025 | Evening Session | Puducherry

As part of our Bone and Joint Week outreach, The POSH - Pondy Ortho Speciality Hospital conducted a Health Awareness Program at Fathima Higher Secondary School, Puducherry.

Our medical team, led by Dr. Senthamizh Selvan, educated students on the following important topics:

  • Importance of bone and joint health in teenagers
  • Basic first aid for injuries and fractures
  • Healthy lifestyle and nutrition tips
The session was informative and interactive. We sincerely thank the management, faculty, and students of Fathima Higher Secondary School for their enthusiastic participation and support in promoting community health awareness.

Health Awareness Program – Vidya Mandir Higher Secondary School

7th August 2025 | Puducherry

The POSH - Pondy Ortho Speciality Hospital proudly conducted a health awareness program at Vidya Mandir Higher Secondary School as part of our Bone and Joint Week initiatives.

Our team, including Dr. Senthamizh Selvan, interacted with students to raise awareness on:

  • Bone and joint health
  • Injury prevention
  • Basic first aid practices
The program was well-received by students and staff. We extend our heartfelt thanks to the school management for their warm hospitality and cooperation in making this initiative a success.

Together, let's build a healthier future for our youth!

Free Medical Camp – Cluny School

As part of our community outreach initiatives, The POSH - Pondy Ortho Speciality Hospital organized a Free Medical Camp at Cluny School.

The camp focused on early detection of orthopaedic issues, general health screening, and medical guidance for students and staff.

The initiative reflects our ongoing commitment to accessible healthcare and preventive medical services for the community.

Free medical camp at Cluny school

Free Medical Camp – Good Shepherd High School

Senthanatham, Villiyanur, Puducherry

The POSH - Pondy Ortho Speciality Hospital successfully conducted a Free Medical Camp at Good Shepherd High School, Senthanatham, Villiyanur.

Our medical team provided health check-ups, orthopaedic consultations, and awareness on bone and joint health to students and staff.

We thank the school management and local community for their support and participation in making this medical camp impactful and meaningful.
